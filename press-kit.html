Full bio

DJ Velvet Grrl, LLC is a Portland Metro mobile DJ, photo booth and MC service. She blends hours of seamless music across every genre for afternoon gatherings, corporate events, nonprofit fundraisers, dinner parties, retirement celebrations, birthday and holiday parties, and weddings. Fully insured and easy to work with, she shapes the music around your guests: send a few must-plays and do-not-plays, and she handles the rest. “I learn what you’re envisioning and make music a feature of your event.”
